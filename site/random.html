THE GREAT SOCK VANISHING

a scientific investigation (kinda)

Hypothesis time

Every laundry cycle, exactly one sock escapes. Where does it go? Leading theories from our imaginary research lab:

  1. Dryer Dimension — a pocket universe behind the lint trap that only accepts mismatched footwear.
  2. Sock Migration — they reunite under the couch with the remote control and a single Lego brick.
  3. Fashion Underground — rogue socks start secret clubs and refuse to pair on principle.
  4. Quantum Laundry — observing the sock collapses the waveform into “missing.”

Until peer review catches up, we recommend labeling both socks with matching stickers shaped like spirals. Spiral energy is strong on this website.
