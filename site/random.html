> CASEFILE — the great sock vanishing

Every laundry cycle, exactly one sock escapes. Working hypotheses:

  1. Dryer Dimension — pocket universe behind the lint trap; only accepts mismatched footwear.
  2. Sock Migration — reunite under the couch with the remote and one Lego.
  3. Fashion Underground — rogue socks refuse to pair on principle.
  4. Quantum Laundry — observing the sock collapses the waveform into “missing.”

Mitigation: label both socks with matching spiral stickers. Spiral energy remains strong.

> FACT — Marshall McLuhan · grave · tetrad

Herbert Marshall McLuhan (1911–1980) was a Canadian media theorist who taught that the medium is the message — the form of a technology reshapes society more than the content it carries. He coined “the global village,” advised on TV-era culture, and treated media as environments you live inside, not pipes you send memos through.

His four laws of media (the tetrad) ask of every tech:

  1. Enhance — What does it amplify or intensify?
  2. Obsolesce — What does it push aside?
  3. Retrieve — What older pattern does it bring back?
  4. Reverse — What happens when it is pushed to extreme?

Example sketch for smartphones: enhance access & presence; obsolesce paper maps and landlines; retrieve the village gossip network at planetary scale; reverse into distraction, surveillance, and always-on anxiety. The grave plaque’s line — the truth shall make you free — reads differently when the medium that delivers “truth” is the thing rewriting your nervous system.
