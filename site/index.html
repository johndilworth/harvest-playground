🐢 Turtle Power: They Live FOREVER (almost)

Turtles are the ultimate slow-and-steady champs. Some giant tortoises easily celebrate their 100th birthday, and a few legendary ones — like Jonathan of St. Helena — have lived past 190 years. Their secret mix includes a sturdy shell, a chill metabolism that barely sips energy, and a lifestyle of munching greens instead of racing around. Freshwater turtles often live several decades in the wild, while sea turtles can cruise the oceans for 50–80+ years if they dodge boats and nets. Scientists think their DNA is unusually good at repairing damage, which is why a turtle with a baseball cap (see left) looks ready for another century of high-fives.

🛋️ Awkward Couch Corner SOS

That weird gap where two couches kiss? Don't panic. Try these ideas:

  • Jungle Tower — a tall plant + string lights turns the void into a mini rainforest.
  • Reading Nook — pile cushions, a little lamp, and a stack of comics. Instant hideout.
  • Pet Palace — a soft bed and toys; your cat/dog will claim the throne.
  • Art Corner — a tiny easel or rotating doodle board for whoever sits nearest.
