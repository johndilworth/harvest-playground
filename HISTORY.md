# Harvest Playground — History

A mostly empty web page that the team grows one sticky at a time. Leave a sticky on the current cycle's board in
Lucid; every day at **5pm MT** the stickies are harvested, every safe request is applied and merged automatically,
and a new cycle board is ready by morning. Rules: [HARVEST.md](HARVEST.md) · item-level detail: [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
